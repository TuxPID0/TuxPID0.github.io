I'm a high school student and self-taught security learner focused on Blue Team and SOC operations. This is my blog, where I publish incident reports, SIEM investigations, PCAP analyses, and notes on what I learn.

## Current Focus

I am building my practical and theoretical foundation through hands-on labs and structured coursework:

* **SOC Operations:** Practicing log parsing, alert triage, and SIEM analysis (Splunk) through TryHackMe's *SOC Level 1* path and SOC Simulator scenarios.
* **Core Fundamentals:** Completing the *Google Cybersecurity Certificate* (Linux, networking, SQL, and Python automation).
* **Theory & Concepts:** Preparing for the *CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)* exam to validate core security concepts.
* **Hands-on Investigation:** Working toward *HTB CDSA (Certified Defensive Security Analyst)* for practical SOC investigation and incident reporting.
